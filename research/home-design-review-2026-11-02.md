# CalcsDesk home-page competitor design review

Observed 2 November 2026. Scope: qualitative review of six live home/calculator entry pages through browser accessibility state and desktop screenshots, with primary-site text retrieval. This is a focused design review, not the full P2 eight-market SERP audit, a mobile competitor audit, a calculator accuracy test or measured conversion/performance research. No competitor data or assets are reused.

| Page reviewed | Observed strength | Opportunity for CalcsDesk | Design response |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Calculator.net](https://www.calculator.net/) | Search and many directly named tool links; categories are apparent. | Scientific calculator occupies the hero despite a money visitor's intent; directory is visually dense. | Keep direct named calculator cards, but make pay/tax and country selection the home-page task. |
| [Omni Calculator](https://www.omnicalculator.com/) | Very prominent search and a legible category matrix. | Breadth is its positioning; a regional money site needs jurisdiction context earlier. | Search plus full country names; no unsubstantiated inventory counters or popularity labels. |
| [The Salary Calculator](https://www.thesalarycalculator.co.uk/salary.php) | Specific pay tasks, Scotland setting, tax year, and advanced option groups. | In the observed viewport a large blank area separated the introduction from salary input. | Reduce hero height and expose the entry control immediately. Reserve complex inputs for the actual tool. |
| [Paycalculator Australia](https://paycalculator.com.au/) | Salary and pay-cycle entry, advanced settings, multi-period result summary, print/share actions. | The observed desktop page had advertising at the bottom and a floating video; the result summary follows the inputs. | Keep a clear input-to-summary relationship and expandable advanced settings in future tools. Use a focused tool finder on the home page. |
| [Wealthsimple Canada](https://www.wealthsimple.com/en-ca/tool/tax-calculator) | Province selection first; labelled inputs and grouped results; clean typography and contextual help. | Corporate product navigation competes with the calculation task. | Region-aware finder, restrained navigation, explicit sources/methodology access. |
| [SmartAsset paycheck](https://smartasset.com/taxes/paycheck-calculator) | Concrete paycheck terminology, state directory, detailed breakdown and related tax tools. | The observed first viewport leads with an explanatory overview and the calculator is further down. | Lead with calculator discovery; move longer method explanations below the tool directory. |

## Conclusions applied to all nine

1. Literal headings name pay, tax, benefits or calculators; the tagline supports the main message.
2. Controls and the first useful tool appear sooner. No decorative illustration competes with the task.
3. Country names are readable instead of relying on two-letter codes. Tool cards expose the intended region next step.
4. Options 2, 5, 7, 8 and 9 include a working local tool finder with country and task selection. It recommends a planned launch tool, never fabricates a result.
5. Sample annual/monthly arithmetic panels are replaced with a jurisdiction-aware start panel and visible methodology link.
6. All tool cards open a preview explaining the planned URL and the official source to be checked during implementation. Source links are navigation, not a claim that tax data has been verified.
7. Preserve original layouts in round-1/ and re-render desktop, mobile and dark previews.

## Recommendation

**1 first, 4 second, 7 third.** Option 1 offers the broadest useful entry without requiring prior knowledge of country taxonomy. Option 4 makes the regional proposition unusually clear. Option 7 is strongest for returning visitors, but its desktop density makes it a less safe default home page. Recommended combination: option 1's search and hierarchy, option 4's full-name country navigation, and option 7's context-aware start panel on country hubs. These are design judgments, not observed conversion outcomes.
