# Canada 2026 federal bracket foundation

Checked 2 November 2026. Foundation only: no public route, catalogue release or completed Canadian income-tax calculator. Human review and two applicable official worked examples remain pending.

[CRA's 2026 rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html) explicitly applies to 2026 income and the return filed in 2027. Its five federal bands end at CAD 58,523, 117,045, 181,440 and 258,482, then remain unlimited; rates are 14%, 20.5%, 26%, 29% and 33%. Only the income within each band receives its rate. [CRA's January 2026 payroll formulas](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4127-payroll-deductions-formulas/t4127-jan/t4127-jan-payroll-deductions-formulas-computer-programs.html) independently corroborate those thresholds/rates. The engine sums bracket slices rather than adopting rounded payroll constants, and makes no payroll claim.

The primary URL is mutable despite its current 2026 heading, so metadata fixes the modeled year, effective calendar-year window and verification date. Later updates require source review. Inputs are already ordinary taxable income, not gross wages. A technical cap of CAD 10 million and two-decimal input precision protect arithmetic; these are product limits, not legal limits. Integer cent/basis-point products are summed before one half-up cent rounding. Band displays round independently. This component display convention is not a claim to implement T1 reporting/rounding rules.

No directly applicable complete 2026 before-credit worked example was verified. [CRA's educational return page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/educational-programs/completing-basic-return.html) distinguishes bracket calculation, credits and provincial taxes, but its Riku quiz uses the 2025 thresholds and 14.5% rate even though the page update is dated 2026. It must not be counted as a 2026 worked example. Tests use explicitly local arithmetic checkpoints, not fabricated official examples.

This foundation omits the basic personal amount and all other federal credits, Quebec abatement, additional/special federal taxes and alternative minimum tax. It does not compute taxable income from gross income, provincial/territorial tax, CPP/QPP or EI, withholding, total liability, refund or take-home pay. Residency and special income categories are not modeled. A complete product needs defined residency/income scope, applicable deductions/credits and regional rules, sourced worked examples, human review and UI/error/privacy verification. B6 remains incomplete.

The pure data validator checks provenance shape, calendar coverage, official CRA URLs, nonfuture verification, increasing safe integer band bounds and rates, and the pending review/example gates. It cannot establish the legal accuracy of a structurally valid changed rate; independent source review remains necessary.
